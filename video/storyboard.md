# OpenDock ad: storyboard (v2)

30 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, music with on-screen text (no voiceover). Made in Blender: OpenDock's interface
rendered offscreen with sample data (nothing personal), on panels in a 3D scene; glass shots in Cycles,
the rest in Eevee. Copy says "Mac-style" and never uses Apple's names or logos.

| # | Time | Shot | On-screen text | What happens |
|---|------|------|----------------|--------------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | Closed laptop | — | White studio, a closed unbranded laptop with a soft shadow; the lid starts to lift. |
| 2 | 0:03–0:06 | It opens | Meet OpenDock | The lid opens, the screen wakes to OpenDock's desktop and Dock. |
| 3 | 0:06–0:08 | Into the screen | A Mac-style desktop for Windows | The camera pushes through the screen until the bezel leaves the frame. |
| 4 | 0:08–0:11 | Liquid Glass | Glass that really bends light | Macro: the Dock glides over the wallpaper, which bends at the rim. |
| 5 | 0:11–0:14 | Icon styles | Your icons, your colors | A wave along the Dock: Default, Clear, Liquid Glass colors, all pink. |
| 6 | 0:14–0:17 | Finder | A Finder that feels at home | The Finder swings in at an angle; folders change color in turn. |
| 7 | 0:17–0:21 | Multitasking | Tile. Stage. Focus. | Drag to the edge, glass outline, snap; the rest slide into Stage Manager. |
| 8 | 0:21–0:24 | Control Center and Island | Everything one click away | The Island grows with a song; Control Center drops down tile by tile. |
| 9 | 0:24–0:30 | Back out, end card | Free and open source · github.com/leemadov/OpenDock | Pull back out to the laptop on white, three-quarter view; wordmark and link fade in. |
